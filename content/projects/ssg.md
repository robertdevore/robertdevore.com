## Static publishing without hidden machinery
Kujo SSG builds Markdown pages, posts, and custom collections; generates structured metadata, feeds, sitemap, robots, and llms.txt; processes assets; and validates output with repository-backed contracts.

[View Kujo SSG on GitHub](https://github.com/kujolang/ssg)
