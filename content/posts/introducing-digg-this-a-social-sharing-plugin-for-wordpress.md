There are a million social sharing plugins out there. Most of them suck 🤷‍♂️

They either slow your site to a crawl, inject bloated tracking scripts, or just look like they were designed in 2008 and never updated.

So in typical _me_ fashion, I built **[Digg This](https://github.com/robertdevore/digg-this/)** – a modern, fast, and customizable social media sharing plugin for WordPress®.

![Digg This example usage in blue](/assets/legacy-images/digg_this_social_icons_blue_edited.webp)

## What Makes Digg This Different?

  * **No BS, Just Sharing** – No tracking scripts. No third-party analytics slowing down your site. Just clean, lightweight sharing icons.
  * **Looks Damn Good** – Icons are crisp, modern, and don’t look like something ripped from MySpace. (😘 [Tabler](https://tabler.io/icons))
  * **Customizable AF** – Pick the colors, choose the networks, and decide which post types should have sharing buttons.
  * **Supports the Networks You Actually Use** – Twitter (X), Facebook, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, WhatsApp, Email, and even SMS.
  * **Built for Performance** – No unnecessary JavaScript, no heavy CSS. Just a few kilobytes of code to keep things snappy.



## Why I Built This

Every other social sharing plugin either slowed my site down or pissed me off with some dark-pattern upsell nonsense.

I wanted something simple, fast, and not bloated with garbage. And to be honest I never really felt the urge to build a plugin for it.

That is, until I had a conversation about being an old bastard and it reminded me about Digg – _if you’re under 30, go ask AI about it_ 😂

> Remember Stumbleupon and Digg? 😂
> 
> — Cristian Raiber (@CristianRaiber) [February 6, 2025](https://twitter.com/CristianRaiber/status/1887534734135152701?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

So I built **[Digg This](https://github.com/robertdevore/digg-this/)** for myself – and now it’s available for you 🤘

## Features That Matter

  * **Customizable Colors** — Make the icons match your site’s aesthetic.
  * **Per-Post Customization** — Add custom share text and hashtags for each post.
  * **Lightweight and Fast** — No scripts calling home, no unnecessary bloat.
  * **Gutenberg & Classic Editor Support** — Because, believe it or not, not everyone wants to use Gutenberg.
  * **Open Source & Actively Maintained** — No corporate nonsense, just a plugin that does what it says.
