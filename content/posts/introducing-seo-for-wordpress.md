**[SEO for WordPress®](https://github.com/robertdevore/seo-for-wordpress)** is different.

It’s a tool designed for everyone – bloggers, small businesses, developers, and anyone who wants a straightforward way to improve their SEO.

Here’s what makes it stand out:

  * **No Upsells** : This plugin doesn’t lock features behind a paywall. You get everything you need right out of the box.
  * **No AI Gimmicks** : While AI has its place, not everyone wants or needs machine-generated suggestions cluttering their workflow.
  * **Performance First** : It’s lightweight, fast, and doesn’t bloat your website with unnecessary scripts or database entries.

## What You Can Do with SEO for WordPress

**[SEO for WordPress®](https://github.com/robertdevore/seo-for-wordpress)** offers a complete suite of tools to help you optimize your site without hassle.

### General Settings

  * Set your **website name** , **tagline** , and **title separator** for better control of how your pages appear in search results.
  * Customize **social media Open Graph tags** , including titles, descriptions, and images, to ensure your content looks great when shared.



### Advanced Settings

  * **Metadata Removal** : Remove unnecessary metadata from your `<head>` section, including REST API links, shortlinks, and oEmbed links.
  * **Breadcrumbs** : Add breadcrumbs with customizable separators and styling options to improve navigation and search engine visibility.
  * **RSS Feed Customization** : Add custom content before and after each post in your RSS feed, and optionally include featured images.
  * **Sitemaps** : Automatically generate XML sitemaps for all public post types, ensuring search engines can index your content efficiently.
  * **Custom Robots.txt** : Add tailored directives for search engine bots directly from your WordPress dashboard.

![SEO for WordPress® - Settings - Advanced](/assets/legacy-images/seo_for_wordpress_settings_advanced_scaled.webp)

### SEO Metabox

For individual posts, pages, and custom post types, you can:

  * Add **SEO titles** , **meta descriptions** , and **keywords**.
  * Set **canonical URLs** to avoid duplicate content penalties.
  * Mark pages as **noindex** or **nofollow** as needed.



### Archive Meta Fields

Customize meta titles, descriptions, and images for categories, tags, and custom taxonomies. You can also configure noindex and nofollow settings for each archive.

## Getting Started

Using **[SEO for WordPress®](https://github.com/robertdevore/seo-for-wordpress)** is as easy as installing the plugin and configuring your settings:

  1. Go to **Settings > SEO Settings** in your WordPress admin dashboard.
  2. Configure your General and Advanced SEO settings.
  3. Use the SEO metabox on individual posts or pages to add custom metadata.
  4. Customize your breadcrumbs and enable them via shortcode `[seo_breadcrumbs]` or the hook `seo_wp_breadcrumbs_hook`.



With everything in place, your site will be optimized for search engines while maintaining its performance and simplicity.
