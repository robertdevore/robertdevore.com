But now?

If you run a multi-author blog, this is a nightmare.

## **Meet Slop Stopper: The AI Slop Bouncer for Your Blog**

[Slop Stopper](/projects/slop-stopper/) is a free **WordPress® plugin that flags AI-generated bullshit before it gets published**.

Here’s how it works:

  * **Scans all new content for AI-sounding phrases** before it goes live.
  * **Stops non-admin users from publishing slop** – if it detects AI garbage, the post gets saved as a draft, and they get told to try again.
  * **Adds a review panel in the WordPress® dashboard** so you can see all flagged posts in one place.
  * **Lets admins manually flag posts** that slip through, because AI slop is sneaky like that.



It’s like having a built-in **bullshit detector** for your blog.
