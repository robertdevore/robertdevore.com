I’m excited to announce the release of **[Block AI Crawlers](https://github.com/robertdevore/block-ai-crawlers)** , a new WordPress plugin designed to give website owners control over how AI crawlers interact with their site.

This lightweight, user-friendly plugin makes it simple to block 75+ known AI crawlers, directly from your WordPress® dashboard.

![AI crawlers visibility - settings screen for the Block AI Crawlers plugin](/assets/legacy-images/block_ai_trackers_for_wordpress_settings_checkbox.webp)

## Why Block AI Crawlers?

In the era of AI-driven content generation and data scraping, many website owners have expressed concerns about the unrestricted access of AI crawlers to their sites. 

These bots can extract valuable content, sometimes for purposes that might not align with the site owner’s intentions. 

Despite months of discussion in [Trac](https://core.trac.wordpress.org/ticket/60805), this feature has yet to make it into core – and looks like it won’t be happening any time soon 🤷‍♂️

That’s why I decided to step up and make it available immediately for the WordPress community. 

With **[Block AI Crawlers](https://github.com/robertdevore/block-ai-crawlers)** , you can take back control and ensure your content is accessed on your terms.

![Give Me Control - Dwight - The Office - GIF](/assets/legacy-images/give_me_control.webp)

## How It Works

The plugin seamlessly integrates with WordPress® settings:

  1. **Settings Integration** : Navigate to **Settings > Reading**, where you’ll find a new checkbox labeled “Block AI Crawlers.”
  2. **Dynamic Robots.txt Rules** : Once enabled, the plugin adds `Disallow` rules to your `robots.txt` file for over 75 known AI crawlers, including ChatGPT, BingAI, OpenAI, and many more.
  3. **Effortless Updates** : The plugin is kept up-to-date with an automatic update feature that pulls changes from the GitHub repository.



This solution is designed to be accessible for all users – no coding required 😎

## How to Get Started

Getting started with **Block AI Crawlers** is simple:

  1. [Download the plugin](https://github.com/robertdevore/block-ai-crawlers) from the official GitHub repository.
  2. Install and activate the plugin on your WordPress site.
  3. Navigate to **Settings > Reading** and enable “Block AI Crawlers.”
  4. Save your changes, and you’re all set!
