Back in 2018, I built **[Dispensary Details for WooCommerce®](https://github.com/getCannaBiz/dispensary-details-for-woocommerce)** to solve a problem:

WooCommerce® lacked the essential cannabis-specific product details that dispensaries needed. Lab results, strain information, effects, and medical benefits – none of it was natively supported.

At the time, I sold the plugin as part of my work with **[CannaBiz Software](https://github.com/getCannaBiz/)** , helping dispensaries and cannabis retailers add much-needed functionality to their online stores.

But the reality of the cannabis industry, especially when it comes to selling software, has been a challenge.

I was de-banked before it was cool 🤷‍♂️

Between strict payment processing rules, advertising restrictions, and the never ending hoops you have to jump through, it became clear that monetizing a plugin in this space is an uphill battle.

So rather than let it collect dust, I decided to release it for free.

## **Why Dispensary Details for WooCommerce® Matters**

Dispensaries running WooCommerce® often struggle to provide customers with the detailed information they need to make informed purchases. 

**[Dispensary Details for WooCommerce®](https://github.com/getCannaBiz/dispensary-details-for-woocommerce)** solves this by adding cannabis-specific product fields and custom taxonomies to enhance product listings.

### **Product-Specific Enhancements**

The plugin introduces key data points for various cannabis product types:

  * **Flower** – Compound details (THC, CBD, THCA, CBN, CBG, CBA)
  * **Edibles** – THC/CBD per serving, serving details, net weight (g)
  * **Tinctures** – THC mg per serving, CBD mg per serving, ml per serving, servings, net weight (oz)
  * **Topicals** – THC mg, CBD mg, size (oz)
  * **Growers** – Origin, grow time, yield, seeds per unit, clones per unit



Along with these, dispensaries can categorize products using custom taxonomies such as **Aromas, Flavors, Effects, Symptoms, Conditions, Ingredients, Shelf Type, Strain Type, and Vendors.**

![Dispensary Details for WooCommerce® Settings screenshot](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_for_woocommerce_settings.webp)

### **Custom WooCommerce Settings**

To further optimize the checkout experience, the plugin also includes settings like:

✅ **Minimum Order Amount** – Prevents checkout if the cart total is below a set amount.  
✅ **Delivery Service Support** – Enables delivery options for patients.  
✅ **Cart and Checkout Redirects** – Ensures customers land on the right pages if their cart is empty or they need to register first.  
✅ **Auto-Complete Orders** – Streamlines order processing by marking orders as completed automatically.  
✅ **Doctor Recommendation System** – Adds verification fields to user registration and checkout, allowing dispensaries to verify medical patients.

## **Why Open Source Matters for the Cannabis Industry**

I’ve been building **open-source software for the cannabis industry since November 2015** , long before mainstream tech companies wanted anything to do with it. 

The cannabis space is still one of the most **heavily restricted markets** for software providers.

Payment processors shut down accounts, advertising is nearly impossible, and closed-source platforms force businesses into rigid systems that don’t truly serve their needs.

That’s why open-source solutions like **[Dispensary Details for WooCommerce®](https://github.com/getCannaBiz/dispensary-details-for-woocommerce)** matter.

They allow dispensaries to **own their data, customize their stores** , and **operate without fear of third-party shutdowns**. 

By releasing this plugin for free, I’m making it easier for dispensaries to use WooCommerce® **without limitations** and without worrying about another paywall.
