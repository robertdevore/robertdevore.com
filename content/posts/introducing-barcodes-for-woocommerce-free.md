I’m thrilled to announce the release of [**Barcodes for WooCommerce®**](https://github.com/robertdevore/barcodes-for-woocommerce/) , a free plugin I’ve developed to make managing WooCommerce® stores more efficient.

By integrating QR code functionality, this tool simplifies how you manage orders and products, while also enhancing your customers’ experience.

Before we even go any further I want to point out that I’ve included a feature that lets admin’s scan a barcode and see order details instantly displayed on the screen 👀

![Barcodes for WooCommerce® \(free\) - Settings - Scan QR Codes](/assets/legacy-images/barcodes_for_woocommerce_settings_scan_barcodes_example_display_905x1024.webp)

This is a feature that the WooCommerce® community has been asking WooCommerce® to include since … checks watch … 2018 🤦‍♂️ ([source](https://woocommerce.com/feature-request/woocommerce-order-barcodes-scanning-directly-to-your-product/))

### 🚀 Key Features of v0.0.1

For this first release, I’ve focused solely on **QR code functionality** to ensure the plugin provides a simple, reliable experience:

![Barcodes for WooCommerce® \(free\) - QR Code in customer email](/assets/legacy-images/barcodes_for_woocommerce_email_qr_code_display.webp)

#### 1\. **Order QR Codes**

  * Automatically generated for all new WooCommerce® orders.
  * Included on the thank-you page, customer emails, and admin order details page.
  * Perfect for quickly accessing and managing orders with a simple scan.

![Barcodes for WooCommerce® \(free\) - QR Code in Product display](/assets/legacy-images/barcodes_for_woocommerce_product_qr_code_display.webp)

#### 2\. **Product QR Codes**

  * Displayed directly below the “Add to Cart” button on product pages.
  * Links to the product itself, making it easy for customers to share or revisit products from their phones.

![Barcodes for WooCommerce® \(free\) - QR Code settings](/assets/legacy-images/barcodes_for_woocommerce_settings_general_settings_1024x521.webp)

#### 3\. **Customizable QR Codes**

  * Easily adjust QR code colors to match your store’s branding.
  * All settings are accessible in a dedicated admin interface.

![Barcodes for WooCommerce® \(free\) - Generate QR Codes in Advanced setings tab](/assets/legacy-images/barcodes_for_woocommerce_settings_regenerate_products.webp)

#### 4\. **Batch Barcode Generation**

  * Generate QR codes for existing products and orders in one click.
  * Automatically generates QR codes for new products and orders moving forward.

### 🛠️ How to Get Started

Getting started with Barcodes for WooCommerce® is straightforward:

  1. **Install and Activate:**  
Download the plugin from the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/robertdevore/barcodes-for-woocommerce/) and activate it in your WordPress dashboard.
  2. **Explore Product QR Codes:**  
Visit any product page on your store to see the QR code displayed below the “Add to Cart” button. Scanning it will take customers directly to that product.
  3. **Use Order QR Codes:**  
Order QR codes are automatically included in: 
     * The thank-you page.
     * Customer order confirmation emails.
     * The admin order details page.
  4. **Customize Your QR Codes:**  
Navigate to **WooCommerce > Barcodes** to customize settings like QR code color to better align with your store’s branding.
  5. **Batch Generate QR Codes:**  
Use the tools in the plugin’s advanced settings to generate QR codes for all your existing products and orders.
