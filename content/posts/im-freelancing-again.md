At the end of 2019, I made a big shift – I took a full-time job for the first time in nearly 15 years.

It was right before my first kid was born, and just a few months later, COVID hit.

Looking back, the timing couldn’t have been better.

For the past five years, I’ve been deep in agency work, handling everything from custom development to security audits, performance optimization, and retainer support. 

It’s been a solid run, but as of exactly one month ago, I’m back to doing my own thing full-time again.

And honestly? That timing feels just as perfect.

**So, what does this mean?**

It means that in addition to continuing my open-source [projects](/projects/), premium plugins like [BoostBox](https://boostboxpopups.com) and [IntelliPress AI](https://intellipress.ai), and apps like [Plugin Pal](https://pluginpal.app), I’m now also taking on client work.
