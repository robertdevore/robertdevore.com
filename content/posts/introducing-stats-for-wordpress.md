The tools we rely on to manage our websites need to reflect our values, both professionally and personally.

Recently, Jetpack, a popular WordPress plugin, has [changed its usage restrictions](https://misophoniafoundation.com/freemium-to-extortion-how-jetpacks-policy-change-hurts-nonprofits-like-ours/), requiring non-profits to pay for features like stats that were once free.

For organizations that have relied on these features for years, this change can feel like a breach of trust and a slap in the face that screams “we need money” more than “we care about you”.

That’s why I’m _dancing-in-my-desk_ excited to introduce **[Stats for WordPress®](https://github.com/robertdevore/stats-for-wordpress/)** , a simple yet powerful analytics plugin designed to put control back into _your_ hands.

With this free plugin, you can track your site’s traffic, referrers, and most popular pages – all without paying unnecessary fees or handing over your data to shady third-party services.

![Stats for WordPress® - Free Analytics plugin](/assets/legacy-images/stats_for_wordpress_screenshot.webp)

## Why I Built Stats for WordPress®

As a software developer and long-time WordPress® community member, I’ve always believed in the power of open source tools to empower individuals and organizations.

When I saw Jetpack’s policy shift, I knew there was a better way – a much better way.

A solution that didn’t force users into paying for features they’ve grown accustomed to and that didn’t compromise data ownership.

**[Stats for WordPress®](https://github.com/robertdevore/stats-for-wordpress)** is my response.

It’s lightweight, respects your data, and offers the essential analytics features you need, directly within your WordPress® dashboard.

![Stats for WordPress® - bar chart and table view](/assets/legacy-images/stats_for_wordpress_screenshot_with_tables.webp)

## What Stats for WordPress® Can Do

**Stats for WordPress®** offers all the essential tools you need to understand your site’s performance:

  * **Track Unique Visits and Page Views** : Get detailed insights into how many people are visiting your site and which pages they’re viewing.
  * **See Top Referrers** : Analyze where your traffic is coming from, excluding your own site to keep the data meaningful.
  * **Focus on What Matters** : The plugin excludes bots, crawlers, and non-content requests (like admin pages or assets), giving you a clean and accurate dataset.
  * **404 Page Tracking** : Identify broken links or missing content by logging visits to 404 pages.
  * **Export Your Data** : Download your stats in a CSV file for further analysis or archiving.
  * **No More Vendor Lock-in** : Your data stays with you, stored securely in your WordPress® database.

## How to Get Started

  1. **Download and Install the Plugin**  
Head over to the [Stats for WordPress® GitHub page](https://github.com/robertdevore/stats-for-wordpress/) and download the plugin.
  2. **Activate the Plugin**  
Upload the plugin to your WordPress site and activate it from the Plugins menu.
  3. **View Your Stats**  
Navigate to the new **Stats** menu in your WordPress admin dashboard to view traffic insights, top pages, and referrers.
  4. **Export Data (Optional)**  
Use the “Download CSV” button to save your stats for offline analysis.

## Looking Ahead: Future Plans for Stats for WordPress®

This is just the beginning.

Here are some features I’m planning to add in future releases:

  * **Real-Time Stats** : See live traffic data as it happens.
  * **Customizable Reports** : Tailor the data you see to match your goals.
  * **Role-Based Access** : Control who can view and manage your site’s analytics.
  * **Enhanced Visualization** : Add more charts and graphs for deeper insights.
  * **Custom Tracking** : Enable tracking for specific events, such as form submissions or downloads.
  * **Multi-Site Compatibility** : Extend support for WordPress multisite networks.



If there’s a feature you’d love to see, let me know! I want to build a tool that meets your needs 🙏

## Why Choose Stats for WordPress®?

There are plenty of analytics tools out there, but few align with the values of the WordPress® community – freedom, simplicity, and control.

**Stats for WordPress®** is different:

  * **No Subscription Fees** : Unlike Jetpack, Stats for WordPress® is completely free and open source.
  * **Respect for Your Data** : Your stats are stored on your own server, not handed over to third-party services.
  * **Lightweight and Fast** : Designed with performance in mind, it won’t slow down your site.
