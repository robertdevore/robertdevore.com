## Part 2: Kevin Michael Geary’s Long Pattern of Industry-Hopping and Reputation Management

Kevin Michael Geary’s current behavior in the WordPress community isn’t new.

It follows a **long-established pattern** :

  * Enter a new industry.
  * Build an audience using fear-based marketing and absolutist positioning.
  * Create paid “inner circles” or premium memberships around his personal brand.
  * Attack dissenters or critics as “haters” or “low-quality individuals.”
  * Eventually flame out, blame the industry, and move on while claiming the industry couldn’t survive without him.



This cycle has repeated itself for **over a decade**.

### Early Career: “The Money Hawk” and “Six Figure Grind”

Kevin Geary’s first major digital pivot was promoting **lifestyle businesses** through brands like _The Money Hawk_ and _Six Figure Grind_.

The message was always the same:

> Escape the rat race. Build passive income. Follow my blueprint.

He leaned heavily on promising success formulas and positioning himself as a savvier guide than others.

Sound familiar?

### Health and Fitness Pivot: “Rebooted Body”

Around 2012–2013, Kevin Geary pivoted again, this time into the health and fitness space with **Rebooted Body**.

Again, the tactics were recognizable:

  * Frame the industry as broken and corrupt.
  * Position himself as the outsider who “really gets it.”
  * Sell lifestyle programs, coaching, and paid memberships.



As criticism mounted, Kevin blamed the industry, blamed the audience, and ultimately exited, framing it as the fitness industry’s loss.

The same **grievance-centered marketing** he uses now was fully visible back then ([source](https://x.com/deviorobert/status/1912310724652597522))

### The Web Development Pivot: “Digital Ambition”

By 2020, Kevin Michael Geary had pivoted once again – running his **Digital Ambition** business, while being the COO of Haman Creative which he promoted in the footer of his Digital Ambition website.

Here, he moved into web strategy & coaching, initially promoting **Divi** in his preferred tech stack.

_(Yes, Divi – the builder he now mocks openly.)_

At this time, he was positioning himself not as a WordPress development expert, but as a business coach for web professionals – selling, again, a lifestyle-focused narrative of success and freedom.

It wasn’t until 2021 that Kevin shifted _again_ … this time aggressively embedding himself in the **Oxygen** and later **Bricks** builder communities, using familiar tactics:

  * Establish an “inner circle” of paid loyalists.
  * Declare himself the standard-bearer for quality.
