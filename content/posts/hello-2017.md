This isn't your average "2017 Goals" article with a list of bullshit and nonsense that I know damn well gets forgotten within the first couple of weeks into January. There's no exhaustive list of _totally awesome shit I am going to accomplish_ or overly detailed blabbering about what 2016 taught me. No resolutions. No fucking around. Just consistent work.

> Small movements. Big picture. 🔑 \-- Robert DeVore (@deviorobert) [November 1, 2016](https://twitter.com/deviorobert/status/793438843118682112?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw)

I'll be narrowing my focus on growing [WP Dispensary](https://www.wpdispensary.com/) as a product and a business this upcoming year, building off of the [1 year birthday](https://twitter.com/i/moments/802554892435025926) the plugin just had in November, and starting early with the recent release of [WooConnect](https://www.wpdispensary.com/downloads/wooconnect-for-woocommerce/) for WooCommerce. Because this isn't a "new years resolution"; it's a way of life. This has been my entire thought process for the last few months, and I'm doubling down on it in 2017. **Goal:** _Do less, better._
