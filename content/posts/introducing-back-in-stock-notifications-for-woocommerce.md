Today I am super excited to introduce the release of my newest **free** plugin – **[Back In Stock Notifications](https://github.com/robertdevore/back-in-stock-notifications/)** 🚀

This is a 100% free plugin for WooCommerce® that lets your customers stay connected with products they love while providing you with meaningful insights into product demand.

Whether you’re a developer helping clients streamline their eCommerce operations, a WooCommerce® store owner seeking to understand customer preferences, or a business owner looking for a simple way to keep high-demand products in the spotlight, _Back In Stock Notifications_ is built with you in mind.

![Back in Stock Notifications for WooCommerce® Dashboard](/assets/legacy-images/back_in_stock_notifications_for_woocommerce_dashboard_scaled.webp)

## Why Back In Stock Notifications?

From an eCommerce perspective, out-of-stock products can mean missed revenue and frustrated customers. 

And by allowing customers to opt into real-time notifications when their favorite items are restocked, this plugin turns a typical eCommerce pain point into an opportunity to engage and retain customers.

It’s also a direct competitor to the Back in Stock Notifications plugin from Woo that they charge $59/year for (at the time of this writing).

It was deliberately chosen as a plugin to build because it allows some of the 20,000 customers ([screenshot](/images/woo_back_in_stock_notificartions_active_installs.webp)) to have a free alternative that does exactly what you need 🤘

[Back In Stock Notifications](https://github.com/robertdevore/back-in-stock-notifications/) provides:

  * **Increased Conversions** : By inviting customers to sign up for notifications, you’re turning interest into action, even when stock levels fluctuate.
  * **Demand Insights** : The BISN dashboard provides visibility into the most wanted and most overdue items, helping you make data-backed inventory and marketing decisions.
  * **Customer-Centric Engagement** : Keep customers informed with a personalized email when the product they love becomes available again.

![Back in Stock Notifications for WooCommerce® Waitlist Screen](/assets/legacy-images/back_in_stock_notifications_for_woocommerce_waitlist_scaled.webp)

## Key Features

### Automated Notifications

Back In Stock Notifications integrates natively with WooCommerce® to capture customer emails for out-of-stock items and then sends automatic emails when these products are restocked. 

It’s a true “set it and forget it” feature designed to streamline the customer notification process.

### Real-Time Demand Analytics

The plugin dashboard includes visual insights to highlight your most in-demand products. Using real-time data, store owners can see:

  * **Most Wanted** : Products with the highest demand.
  * **Most Overdue** : Out-of-stock products with the longest wait times.
  * **Most Signed-Up** : Historical data on top sign-up products, helping you pinpoint your biggest revenue drivers.



This data doesn’t just inform inventory decisions – it helps you design marketing campaigns around your top products.

![Back in Stock Notifications for WooCommerce® Email Example](/assets/legacy-images/back_in_stock_notifications_for_woocommerce_waitlist_notification_email_851x1024.webp)

### Customizable Email Templates

Keeping on-brand is crucial, especially for customer communications. The Back In Stock Notifications plugin for WooCommerce® provides customizable HTML and plain text templates.

Developers can copy these templates to a theme directory to apply custom styling, making it easy to create emails that match the brand’s tone, design, and personality.

### Automatic Updates with Plugin Update Checker

This plugin uses [Plugin Update Checker](https://github.com/YahnisElsts/plugin-update-checker), which makes sure you’ll receive every update, right from within your WordPress® dashboard.

This means no more manual downloads or installations – just an easy, seamless update experience the way you deserve 🤘

## Getting Started with Back In Stock Notifications

### Step 1: Install the Plugin

Installing **Back In Stock Notifications** is simple. 

Download the plugin [from GitHub](https://github.com/robertdevore/back-in-stock-notifications/), upload it to your WordPress® site, and activate it. Make sure the WooCommerce® plugin is active, as this plugin is built to run on WooCommerce-enabled sites.

### Step 2: Configure and Customize

After activating the plugin, configure the email template to match your brand’s style. 

The email templates, located in the plugin’s `templates/emails` directory, are ready to be customized with your brand’s look and feel.

### Step 3: Analyze and Adjust

Once customers start using the waitlist, you’ll see real-time data populate your dashboard. 

Use this insight to adjust inventory, run promotions, or optimize reordering schedules around your highest-demand products.
