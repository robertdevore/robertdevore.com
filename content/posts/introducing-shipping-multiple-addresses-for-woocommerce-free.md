I’m thrilled to share my latest plugin that aims to make WooCommerce® even more powerful and versatile.

Introducing **[Shipping Multiple Addresses for WooCommerce®](https://github.com/robertdevore/shipping-multiple-addresses-for-woocommerce)** , the free plugin that simplifies sending items to multiple locations from a single order.

Whether you’re a store owner or a customer, this plugin is here to enhance your WooCommerce® experience.

## Why This Plugin Matters

You and I both know that customers expect flexibility.

Maybe they’re sending gifts to family and friends, or perhaps a business needs to distribute products to multiple offices.

The default WooCommerce® setup handles shipping beautifully for single addresses, but what about scenarios where multiple destinations are involved?

That’s where [Shipping Multiple Addresses for WooCommerce®](https://github.com/robertdevore/shipping-multiple-addresses-for-woocommerce) comes in.

![Shipping Multiple Addresses for WooCommerce® \(free\) - Checkout Assign Addresses screenshot](/assets/legacy-images/shipping_multiple_addresses_for_woocommerce_checkout_assign_addresses.webp)

## Key Features

This plugin is loaded with features to handle the complexity of multi-address shipping seamlessly:

### 1\. **Address Management**

Customers can save and manage multiple shipping addresses directly from their account dashboard.

This makes checkout quicker and more convenient for repeat orders.

![Shipping Multiple Addresses for WooCommerce® \(free\) - Duplicate Cart](/assets/legacy-images/shipping_multiple_addresses_for_woocommerce_duplicate_cart_1024x772.webp)

### 2\. **Cart Duplication**

Need the same item shipped to multiple locations?

With the “Duplicate Cart” button, you can easily replicate cart items.

### 3\. **Custom Delivery Options**

Customers can add delivery notes and select a preferred delivery date for each shipment. These details are saved with the order and accessible to the store admin.

### 4\. **Automatic Order Splitting**

The plugin automatically splits orders into sub-orders based on assigned addresses, making fulfillment and tracking a breeze.

### 5\. **Email Notifications**

Get custom notifications for split and partial orders to keep customers informed every step of the way.

![Shipping Multiple Addresses for WooCommerce® \(free\) - Admin Settings](/assets/legacy-images/shipping_multiple_addresses_for_woocommerce_admin_settings_969x1024.webp)

### 6\. **Admin-Friendly Tools**

Admins can:

  * Highlight sub-orders in the order list.
  * View parent-child relationships between orders.
  * Configure settings for excluded products and categories directly from WooCommerce settings.



## How It Works

### For Customers

  1. **Manage Addresses:** Log in to your WooCommerce® account and navigate to the new “Manage Addresses” section.
  2. **Checkout Flexibility:** Assign specific products to different saved addresses during checkout.
  3. **Submit Your Order:** Once the order is placed, it’s automatically split into sub-orders for each address.

![Shipping Multiple Addresses for WooCommerce® \(free\) - Manage Addresses](/assets/legacy-images/shipping_multiple_addresses_for_woocommerce_admin_settings_1_960x1024.webp)

### For Store Owners

  1. **Setup and Configuration:** Install the plugin, activate it, and configure settings under **WooCommerce > Settings > Shipping > Split Orders Settings**.
  2. **Easy Order Management:** Use the order list to track parent-child order relationships and sub-order statuses.
  3. **Exclusions and Customizations:** Control which products/categories can be excluded from multi-shipping and customize the checkout UI to match your store’s branding.
