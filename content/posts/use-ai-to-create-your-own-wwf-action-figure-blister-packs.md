## Why a JSON prompt?

Simple text prompts are fun. But when you want repeatability, precision, and that “this is exactly what I envisioned” moment – JSON is the move.

Here’s why:

When you write a normal text prompt, you’re leaving a lot up to the model to interpret. 

You might get the right subject, but the style might be off. Or the color’s wrong. Or it forgot the accessories.

But with a **structured JSON prompt** , you’re handing AI a blueprint – not just a nudge.

You’re saying:

  * **This** is the style.
  * **This** is the pose.
  * **These** are the accessories.
  * **This** is how the packaging should look.



It’s like switching from giving someone directions with vague hand gestures to handing them GPS coordinates.
