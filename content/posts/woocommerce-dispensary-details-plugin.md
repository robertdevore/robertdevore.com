I recently built and released a new plugin on **WP Dispensary** that works 100% without [WP Dispensary](https://www.wpdispensary.com).

> "But didn't you just jump in [full time](/going-all-in-with-wp-dispensary/) to build WP Dispensary? Why create something that doesn't work with it?"

I know, right? ![](/assets/legacy-images/tell_us_more.webp)via [giphy](https://www.giphy.com/) There is definitely a reason I chose to build a separate plugin for WooCommerce that works 100% independently from WP Dispensary. Although it may not be that over complicated and well thought out ? **Because it is something companies need, so why not be the one who provides it for them?**![](/assets/legacy-images/kanye_gif_giphy.webp) via [giphy](https://giphy.com) GENIUS ? Actually, I have had people say that clients of theirs use [WooCommerce](https://www.woocommerce.com) already and don't necessarily want to switch over to WP Dispensary. While I hate to hear that since I have an impartial love for **WP Dispensary** , I understand it from a business perspective. So, instead of leaving a need out there, I looked at[WP Dispensary](/wp-dispensary/) and knew I could create a standalone plugin that added the proper details and displayed cleanly within the WooCommerce structure. That's where [**Dispensary Details for WooCommerce**](<https://www.wpdispensary.com/downloads/dispensary-details-for-woocommerce>) was born. A simple need that I want to make sure is taken care of. ![](/assets/legacy-images/john_cusack_gif_giphy_300x158.webp)via [giphy](https://www.giphy.com)

## So what does it do?

What it does is simple; add in custom taxonomies and metaboxes for a variety of details from aroma to vendor, ingredients and compound details. These are added into the back end of WordPress, directly in the Add/Edit page for WooCommerce products. [![](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_for_woocommerce_screenshot_1_300x215.webp)](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_for_woocommerce_screenshot_1_300x215.webp)click to view metaboxes [![](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_for_woocommerce_screenshot_2_300x185.webp)](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_for_woocommerce_screenshot_2_300x185.webp)click to view taxonomies Once you've added in your information into the back end, I've added in a new product tab next to Description and Reviews - Details - which will output your dispensary details in a clean table. [![](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_front_end_table_300x163.webp)](/assets/legacy-images/dispensary_details_front_end_table_300x163.webp)click to view large I utilized one of the tricks in my [custom post types for WordPress](/wordpress-custom-post-types-code-snippets/) post and each of the taxonomies get flushed on activation so your permalinks will work for the archives of `Aromas`, `Edibles`, etc.
