## The Problem: Why WordPress® plugin development benefits from AI

Building a plugin isn’t just about writing code.

It’s about working within a set of of best practices, security constraints, and design decisions. 

It’s about reinventing the wheel, over and over and over again.

I know this because I’ve been there. 

Over the course of Q4 2024, I’ve built dozens of [WordPress® plugins](/projects/), often with the help of AI (shocker, right? 😂)

And while AI accelerated that process for me, I realized something crucial: speed alone isn’t enough.

What developers need is a tool that doesn’t just generate code, but generates _good_ code – the type of code that’s clean, secure, and ready for production.

That’s the problem [Plugin Pal](https://pluginpal.app) was designed to solve.

## The Solution: Plugin Pal and the power of AI

Plugin Pal isn’t just another little AI tool. 

It’s a carefully crafted system that combines AI with a deep understanding of WordPress® development (hello, 20+ years experience).

At its core is **GPT-4o** , and unlike its predecessors, GPT-4o doesn’t just write code anymore. 

The difference between the two is night and day for the quality it produces.

And because of that, **Plugin Pal** understands context, adheres to best practices, and produces plugins that are modular, secure, and localization-ready.

[![Plugin Pal - AI powered plugin generator for WordPress®](/assets/legacy-images/plugin_pal_wordpress_plugin_generator.webp)](https://pluginpal.app)

But here’s the thing: [Plugin Pal](https://pluginpal.app) doesn’t just rely on AI.

It’s built on a completely modern tech stack that ensures it’s fast, scalable, and developer-friendly. 

[Flask](https://flask.palletsprojects.com/en/stable/) powers the web app, [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/) for the relational DB and [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) \+ [Flowbite](https://flowbite.com/) blocks for the front-end design.

It’s hosted on [Heroku](https://heroku.com/), so it’s always available when you need it.

And it’s designed with a single goal in mind: give WordPress® plugin developers a killer head start, so you can focus on the creative, meaningful parts of your work.

**Plugin Pal** doesn’t just spit out simple code. It generates WordPress® plugins that are:

  * **Secure** : Every plugin includes built-in security measures.
  * **Localization-ready** : Every plugin comes with a `.pot` file for easy translation.
