## Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MVP development, timelines, and the process. 

How quickly can you deliver my MVP?

Most MVPs are delivered within 4-8 weeks depending on complexity. The Starter MVP takes just 4 weeks, while more complex Enterprise MVPs need 8 weeks. I provide weekly demos to keep you updated on progress.

What's included in the fixed price?

Everything needed for a complete MVP: planning, design, development, testing, deployment, and 30-day post-launch support. No hidden costs or surprise fees. You own all code and assets upon completion.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance after launch?

Yes! All packages include 30 days of post-launch support. After that, I offer flexible maintenance plans starting at $500/month for bug fixes, security updates, and minor feature additions.

Can you scale the MVP to a full product later?

Absolutely! MVPs are built with scalable architecture from day one. I can extend features, add integrations, and scale infrastructure as your user base grows. Many clients continue working with me for their full product development.

What if I need changes during development?

Minor adjustments are included in the fixed price. For major scope changes, I'll provide a transparent quote for additional work. Weekly demos help catch any course corrections early in the process.
