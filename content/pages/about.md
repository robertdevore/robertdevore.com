## I build systems that explain themselves

My current work centers on Kujo and the tools around it: language and runtime infrastructure, static publishing, package and release workflows, agent orchestration, retrieval, evaluation, telemetry, and evidence-rich engineering loops.

The common thread is inspectability. Inputs should be visible, boundaries explicit, and outputs useful to the next person—or agent.

## Clarity, context, control

- **Clarity:** readable contracts, deliberate interfaces, and fewer places for meaning to hide.
- **Context:** useful metadata, durable artifacts, and handoffs that preserve why a decision was made.
- **Control:** bounded automation, explicit permissions, deterministic checks, and human decisions where they matter.
